How progression works

Rate each exercise Light, Solid, or Cooked. After the workout, RepState recommends your next working weight.

LightRoom to add
SolidRight on target
CookedAdjust the load

During a workout

Pick up where you stopped. Your exercise, completed sets, working weights, effort ratings, and rest countdown remain until you end the session.

Rest on your terms. Choose no timer, 60 or 90 seconds, or 2 or 3 minutes. Dismiss it whenever you are ready.

Undo accidental taps. Reverse the last completed set before you continue.

Keeping your data

Sync when you want. Create an account and bring your existing settings, plan, weights, sessions, and effort ratings with you.

Keep a full backup. Download the same data any time as JSON.
