    Good training

    The four rules

    Keep two clean reps in reserve

    Stop the set while you could still perform about two more repetitions with the same form.

    Rest long enough

    Take 90–120 seconds between working sets. Start the next set when breathing feels settled.

    Progress in small steps

    When every set reaches the top of its rep range, add the smallest available weight next time.

    Control every repetition

    No bouncing, forced repetitions, maximal testing or grinding to failure.

    Stop for sharp pain, dizziness or unusual shortness of breath. Ask gym staff or a clinician before continuing if something feels wrong.
