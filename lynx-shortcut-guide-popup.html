iPhone Shortcut

Convert and share directly from your music app

Install it once. After that, Lynx lives in the Share menu inside Spotify and Apple Music.

  1. 1 Install the ShortcutApple opens Shortcuts and asks you to add it.
  2. 2 Tap Share on any songUse Spotify or Apple Music, then open its Share menu.
  3. 3 Choose Share with LynxThe matching link appears, ready to send.
