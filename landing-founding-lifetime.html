Why it’s different

Spawning agents is easy. Coordinating the build is the work.

thinkpool keeps terminals, handoffs, reviews, and visual proof in one visible Ensemble. One developer can run it. Invite a partner and both people can watch and steer the same live work without transferring context.

Cascade

Split the work. Keep control.

A conductor investigates, sends defined slices to visible worker lanes, then collects and verifies what comes back.

Review

Make every result prove itself.

Reviewer lanes try to break the work before it lands. Builds, tests, evidence, and handoffs stay in the same room.

Design

Edit the working interface.

Turn a verified preview into an editable Design surface. Changes return to the producing lane and come back as fresh desktop and mobile proof.
