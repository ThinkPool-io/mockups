Useful things to know.

Which music links does Lynx support?

Public track and album links from Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music.

Do I need to connect a music account?

No. Lynx does not require an account, a subscription login, or access to your music library.

What if there is no exact match?

Lynx provides a clearly labelled search link rather than guessing and returning the wrong version.

Can Lynx transfer a playlist or library?

Not currently. Lynx is designed for quickly sharing individual tracks and albums between listeners.
