About

I built SlopStream to make public TikTok and Instagram posts easy to share between friends. The recipient sees a single clean post with full author credits, outside of the app that is engineered to keep you endlessly scrolling.

It is a small passion project, not a commercial service. Anyone can use it within the hard daily limits: 100 successful conversions across SlopStream and five per IP address each day.

Submitted links may be sent to TikTok, Instagram, and a resolver service to load public media. Hosting providers may temporarily log IP addresses, browser details, and request times.
SlopStream has no accounts, cookies, analytics, or ads.
