Why it exists

For people who just want the post

Sometimes the right video is inside an app the recipient does not have. SlopStream lets parents, relatives, mixed-platform group chats, and privacy-conscious recipients open just the post instead of entering a feed.

How it works

Two ways to share

Paste a link on SlopStream to create a browser link you can send anywhere.

On iPhone, the optional Share with SlopStream Shortcut does the same thing from the TikTok or Instagram Share menu.

Boundaries

What to expect

SlopStream works with supported public posts. It cannot make private, removed, region-restricted, unsupported, or otherwise unavailable content viewable.

Creator credit stays attached
Supported posts keep attribution and a route back to the original creator and platform.
No video downloads
SlopStream creates a browser view for the original public post rather than downloading it.
No SlopStream accounts, analytics, or ads
Submitted links may still be sent to TikTok, Instagram, and a resolver service. TikTok’s official player may use TikTok cookies. Hosting providers may temporarily log IP addresses, browser details, and request times.
Fallback playback has limits
Public links and official playback are unlimited. If TikTok’s official player fails, native fallback playback is limited to 100 plays overall and 5 per connection each day.
