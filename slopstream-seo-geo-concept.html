Share public TikTok and Instagram videos without the apps.

SlopStream turns a supported public post into one clean browser link. The recipient can watch the post with creator attribution, without opening TikTok or Instagram and without falling into a feed.

How it works

A post is not a feed.

SlopStream is deliberately narrow. It is a relay for a public post, not a new place to browse social media.

Boundaries, stated plainly

What SlopStream does and does not promise.

It works with supported public TikTok and Instagram posts. It preserves creator attribution. It does not turn private, removed, region-restricted, or otherwise unavailable posts into viewable media.

  • Public onlyPrivate posts and content that a source platform will not provide cannot be opened.
  • Creator creditEach supported post keeps a link and attribution to its original creator and platform.
  • No account layerSlopStream has no accounts, analytics, or ads.
  • PlaybackPublic links and TikTok’s official playback are unlimited. If the official TikTok player fails, native fallback playback is limited to 100 plays overall and 5 per connection each day.

Questions people actually ask

FAQ

Short answers, because the product is a small thing.

Can I share TikTok videos without the TikTok app?

For supported public TikTok posts, SlopStream makes a clean browser link so the recipient can open the post without the TikTok app. Some posts may still be unavailable because of source-platform restrictions.

Can I share Instagram videos without the Instagram app?

For supported public Instagram posts, SlopStream creates a browser route that lets a recipient open the post without installing Instagram. It does not bypass private or restricted content.

Does SlopStream download videos?

No. SlopStream is for sharing a supported public post through a clean browser link. It keeps creator attribution and the original source visible.

What data does SlopStream collect?

SlopStream has no accounts, analytics, or ads. Submitted links may be sent to TikTok, Instagram, and a resolver service to load public media. TikTok’s official player may use TikTok cookies, and hosting providers may temporarily log IP addresses, browser details, and request times.

Why can a video fail to open?

The original post may be private, deleted, region-restricted, unsupported, or unavailable from its platform. TikTok official playback can also fail. In that case, native fallback playback has the stated shared and per-connection limits.

About SlopStream

A small relay for sharing one thing well.

SlopStream exists for the ordinary moment when a public TikTok or Instagram post is worth sending, but the recipient should not have to enter the app to see it.

Mission

Keep the handoff intact

A link should carry the post, its creator, and enough context to watch. It should not quietly carry a feed, a sign-up demand, or a new account requirement.

How it operates

A browser route for public posts

SlopStream sends a submitted public link to TikTok, Instagram, and a resolver service as needed to load media. It then presents the supported post in a browser.

Privacy

No accounts, analytics, or ads

SlopStream does not operate an account system or ads. TikTok’s official player may use TikTok cookies, and hosting providers may temporarily log IP addresses, browser details, and request times.

Limitations

Public and supported is the boundary

Private, removed, restricted, or unsupported posts cannot be opened. Public links and official playback are unlimited. TikTok native fallback is limited to 100 plays overall and 5 per connection each day.
