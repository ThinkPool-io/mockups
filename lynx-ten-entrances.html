Lynx

Share the song
across platforms

Sometimes the right song is inside a music app the recipient does not use. Lynx turns a public track or album into one link that works across Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music.

Two ways to share

Paste a supported music link to convert it.

On iPhone, the optional Share with Lynx Shortcut converts directly from the Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube Music Share menu.

What to expect

Private, removed, region-restricted, or otherwise unavailable music may not convert.

Direct matches are verified
Catalog evidence is checked before a direct result is shown.
Search is safer than guessing
Ambiguous editions, regions, or catalog data open a focused Search instead of risking the wrong release.
No Lynx accounts, ads, or music-library access
Successful conversions are counted without recording music metadata. A keyed network fingerprint prevents duplicate event delivery and expires with its signed receipt. Lynx uses no cookies or behavioral profiles.
Failed-match diagnostics are temporary
IP addresses, browser details, request times, and submitted music links are logged for security. A link and its catalog metadata may be kept for up to 14 days so a failed match can be reproduced and fixed. Link records are stored separately from visitor details and user identities.
