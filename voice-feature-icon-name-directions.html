Feature identity study

Ten ways to name and mark the voice-to-action feature.

Every direction replaces the plain microphone in the real room composer. The same mark then identifies the saved object agents can act on.

My shortlist
1. thinkpool BriefBest default. Clear now and still works if typed briefs arrive later.
2. thinkpool DropMost memorable. Best if the feature becomes a recurring product ritual.
