Executive summary

What was done. Production thinkpool.io (build 21c9e92e, then 2e7d409b after today's merges) was driven as a signed-out visitor, as the real account, and as a throwaway Free account that created, used and deleted its own room. Five Opus 5.5 lanes measured every public route, the signed-in dashboard, /settings, the room, performance, and every public claim against repo constants and live Supabase config, at 7 viewports (390, 430, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1920). Three Fable 5.1 review lanes then re-measured every finding on prod and killed anything they could: 5 rejected, 14 downgraded, the rest confirmed with the reviewer's own numbers. Every finding below carries a measurement, a selector or quote, a source line, a crop where visual, and the review verdict.

Shipped today (verified on prod): #435 removed the /brief page (307 → /, backup tag backup/brief-page-2026-09-28; API, bridge tools, iPhone Shortcut and migrations left in place pending a phase-B decision) and #436 pinned Playwright to 1.61.0 with a dependabot ignore, because 1.62+ has no macOS 13 browser and the local test gate had silently lost its 4 browser-backed test files.

Nothing else was changed. Fixes wait for your pick.

### B-01 [P1] Modal focus trap and initial focus are dead: focus escapes to the page behind
- **Surface/state:** Every Modal (naming, rename, delete-confirm, quota, invite): focus management
- **Viewports:** all 7 (verified 1440x900 + 390x844 in probe-focus, 7/7 in dash/dialogs)
- **Selector:** `[role="dialog"] (Modal in src/components/redesign/ui.jsx)`
- **Measured:** Naming dialog: Tab order = select, link, Cancel, Start, then body, then nav "Settings" (behind the overlay), then bridge row, then Providers: focus leaves the aria-modal dialog after 4 stops. - **Expected:** aria-modal dialogs move focus inside on open and trap Tab/Shift+Tab (WCAG 2.4.3; the Modal comment itself documents a trap: ui.jsx "H24 focus trap + restore"). A destructive confirm should put initial focus on Cancel.
- **Crop:** `/tmp/tp-audit/B/crops/B-01.png`
